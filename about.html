Real Estate Media With a Personal Standard

My name is Cameron, and Flying Ace's Photography & Drone Services was built on one simple belief: the way a property is presented matters.

Whether it's a home, vacant land, a commercial project, or a business that needs stronger visual content, people make decisions based on what they see first. My goal is to make that first impression clean, professional, and worth paying attention to.

As an FAA Certified Commercial Drone Pilot and retired military veteran, I bring creativity, discipline, and attention to detail to every shoot. I specialize in real estate photography, drone photo and video, listing videos, Zillow 3D walkthroughs, floor plans, land coverage, promotional media, and specialty drone services throughout North Florida and surrounding areas.

More than anything, I care about making the process easy. When you book with me, you're not just hiring someone to take photos — you're hiring someone who understands that your listing, your client, your business, and your reputation are on the line.

Why It Matters to Me

A big reason I push so hard to build this business is my daughter. She reminds me that this is bigger than photos and videos. I'm building something with my name on it — something I can be proud of and something she can see as an example of hard work, pride, and creating something from the ground up.
