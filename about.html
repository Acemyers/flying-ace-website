About Flying Ace's

Real estate media with purpose, precision, and a personal touch.

Flying Ace's was built around one belief: the way a property is presented matters. People make decisions based on what they see first — my job is to make sure that first impression is worth paying attention to.

When you book with me, you are not just hiring someone to show up and take photos. You are hiring someone who treats your listing, your client, and your reputation like they are on the line.

Why I Started This

As an FAA Certified Commercial Drone Pilot and retired military veteran, I bring both creativity and discipline to every shoot. The military taught me to do the job right the first time — real estate media gave me a way to put that into something visual and useful for other people.

Family Is a Big Part of Why I Do This

My daughter is a big part of why I push this hard. I am building something with my name on it — something she can see — and that means every shoot, every client, and every project is a chance to build a reputation worth being proud of.

A Personal Standard on Every Shoot

Starter home, luxury listing, vacant land, or commercial project — I approach every shoot the same way: if this were mine, I would want it shown at its absolute best. I do not deliver “good enough.” I want the photos and videos to make someone stop scrolling and feel like the property is worth seeing in person.

What You Can Expect

Clear communication, reliable scheduling, polished editing, and media delivered in 24–48 hours. From ground-level interiors to aerial drone coverage, I focus on showing the full story of a property — the layout, the details, and the features that make it worth noticing.

The Flying Ace's Standard

I believe good media should do three things:

01

Get Attention.

Your photos and videos should stop people from scrolling.

02

Build Confidence.

Buyers and clients should immediately feel like the property or business is worth a closer look.

03

Make You Look Professional.

Because when your marketing looks better, your brand looks better.

That is the standard I aim for on every project.

Ready When You Are

Let's Make Your Property Stand Out

If you need clean, professional photos, drone coverage, video, 3D walkthroughs, floor plans, or media that helps your property or business get noticed, I would be honored to help.
